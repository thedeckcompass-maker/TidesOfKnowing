## Controlled Step 5B — approved initial-launch quotation operations (1 November 2026)

Authority: Owner Step5B, Sentinel_418aed342e448191b030d08607e249dd; attachment libfile_54d7b227a6fc819192b2d6d84adfa431, Pasted text(20261101-205707).txt, 1 November 2026. This dated addendum preserves all earlier records and the checkpoint269 audit baseline.

Creators submit additional-work enquiries through the existing Studio interface. The stored enquiry retains its creator identity and service-record association. Leilia reviews the request and sends an individual quotation by email specifying scope, price and applicable conditions. Explicit written acceptance of that scope and price is required before additional work or charge authorization. A change to scope or price requires fresh written acceptance. An enquiry, quotation or acceptance never automatically triggers payment, service allocation or additional entitlements.

### Manual operating procedure

1. Review the existing administrator additional-work enquiries view. Retain the stored enquiry ID (the quote ID used by its service-quote page anchor), creator identity, request date, category, working title and original requested outcome. Do not confuse a working title with project-sharing permission. Verify the intended creator's correspondence address through the existing authorized account process; a display name alone is not sufficient. No new account lookup or correspondence is authorized by this documentation step.
2. Associate the correspondence record with that original enquiry ID. Record each proposed quotation's version/date, scope, price and applicable conditions. Preserve the original enquiry and every issued version; do not overwrite previous terms. No validity period, cancellation fee, payment deadline, refund right or new price is introduced here.
3. Leilia sends the versioned proposal by email. Retain the exact sent proposal and the correspondence that identifies its recipient. Obtain explicit written acceptance referencing the applicable version/scope/price. Questions, silence, an enquiry submission or a receipt acknowledgement are not acceptance. Retain the acceptance message and its date with the proposal.
4. If scope or price changes, preserve the previous proposal and acceptance, identify the revised version and obtain fresh written acceptance before undertaking the changed work or authorizing its charge. If a proposal is withdrawn, retain the withdrawal communication and identify which version it concerns. Do not treat an accepted agreement as automatically cancelled or invent withdrawal consequences: those require the applicable agreed conditions/owner decision.
5. Keep accepted scope separate from delivery records: record what was actually delivered and when, linked to the enquiry and accepted version. Acceptance does not mean work has been delivered or payment collected. Keep any separately authorized payment record/reference distinct. Do not manually inflate existing programme allocations to represent additional work.
6. Keep the enquiry reference, versioned proposals, acceptance/revision/withdrawal correspondence, delivery references and separately authorized financial references together in an organized, access-restricted, retrievable existing business-record location. Preserve identities, dates, original messages and version relationships for disputes and possible later migration. This specifies record contents, not a new application schema or repository. The exact existing mailbox/folder or record-system location and responsible backup/retention arrangements have not been established by the evidence reviewed; Leilia must designate them before operating the process. No new retention duration is invented.

### Launch, deferred work and financial gates

- Initial launch: existing enquiry submission, persistence, retry protection and creator/admin readback plus this manual email quotation/written-acceptance process.
- Approved deferred capability: in-app quotation preparation, presentation, revision, acceptance, history and payment integration. Its absence is not an initial-launch defect.
- Approved deferred capability: additional consultation/assessment purchasing. Keep existing buttons disabled, as expressly approved on 24 October at22:22:13UTC (Sentinel_eb3a3a21f5008191b80a25dc9fa17d0e).
- Future owner decisions: additional-service definitions, prices, eligibility, scheduling, expiry, purchase-generated allocations and payment conditions. No defaults inferred from programme inclusions.
- External gate: payment arrangements and any future activation remain separately controlled and accepted. No automated email, payment, grant or service allocation follows from an enquiry or written acceptance.
