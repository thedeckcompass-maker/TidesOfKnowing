## Proposed policy text

Tides of Knowing respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the Tides of Knowing website, Ask Leilia, COMPASS, Deck Creator Studio (DCS), its Community, and related support services.

**[OWNER REVIEW: insert the legal operator’s name, business address and applicable jurisdiction, and confirm the privacy contact below.]**

### Information we collect

When you register, contact us, apply for a programme or request support, we receive the account and contact details you provide, such as your name, email address, Community username and correspondence. We maintain authentication, account status and access records so that you can sign in and use the services available to you.

In DCS, we store the project material you choose to save: ideas, card text, artwork and reference images, guidebook material, project plans, workbook responses and private journal entries. Your project material is separate from anything you deliberately share in the Community. Using the Studio does not transfer ownership of your work to DCS; ownership and licensing are separate from the handling of personal information described here.

When you post in the Studio Community, your username, contribution and relevant pathway badge are visible to eligible Community members. Replies, Scribes, reports and moderation records support Community operation. A report is sent to the authorised moderator rather than published as a Community contribution. Moderator task records include read state, completion time and the person who handled the task; completing a task does not remove its underlying contribution.

If you request confidential editorial input, we receive the saved version, selected text or reference preview, question and context that you explicitly submit. This is a separate conversation with authorised editorial staff. It does not publish your project to the Community or grant artists access to it. We also keep service requests, scheduling correspondence, access grants and the records needed to honour agreed programme allowances.

Our hosting and authentication providers process technical information needed to operate and protect the website, such as request, device, network, security and sign-in information. **[OWNER REVIEW: verify the cookie, analytics and technical-log inventory and retention periods before publication; do not infer that all cookies are optional or that analytics are active.]**

### How we use information

We use this information to provide the website and Studio, authenticate accounts, save and export your work, apply access rights, deliver requested programmes and support, respond to enquiries, arrange agreed sessions, moderate Community contributions, investigate reports, prevent abuse and maintain necessary business and recovery records.

We do not sell or rent your personal information or share it with third parties for their marketing. **[OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW: specify the applicable legal bases and any additional statutory notices after the operator and relevant jurisdictions are confirmed.]**

### Who can access it

Your project is private to your account through the normal Studio access controls until you choose to share material. Authorised staff and service providers may need limited access to operate the service, resolve a support or security issue, or meet a legal obligation. This does not give other creators access to your project.

Confidential editorial material is restricted to the submitting creator and the authorised editorial recipient. A dedicated assistant or other collaborator is not automatically entitled to private editorial or project access. Where a separately agreed service requires a human assistant, we will explain the scope of access and confidentiality arrangements before that work begins. **[OWNER REVIEW: confirm assistant contracts, duties and approved access before representing those arrangements as established.]**

Community contributions are shared within the single Studio Community. Agreements with artists, designers and other people you choose to work with are between you and those people. Information you send them directly is also subject to their own handling practices.

### Providers, payments and international processing

We use Cloudflare for website delivery and application hosting and Supabase for database, authentication and private file storage. These providers process information on our behalf under their service arrangements. Our current DCS database region is Singapore; website delivery and provider support may involve other countries. **[OWNER/LEGAL REVIEW: verify provider contracts, international-transfer arrangements, subprocessors and required disclosures before publication.]**

Where payments are available on the Tides website, Stripe processes payment details. We do not store full payment-card details in the Studio. We may retain the transaction and entitlement information needed to administer a purchase. DCS payment collection is not activated by this policy.

Video, recording and transcription services are not activated by this policy. Before introducing recording or transcription, we will explain the provider, purpose, access, consent arrangements and retention period. This draft does not authorise use of private project material to train AI systems or activate an AI processing service.

We retain information for the purposes described here and any applicable legal or record-keeping requirements. **[OWNER REVIEW: set specific account, project, support, payment, security-log and confidential-editorial periods, including what happens after membership ends. **Proposed hidden-content rule, not yet operating:** hidden Community content will normally be retained for 12 months from the date it was hidden, then permanently deleted under a controlled retention process. Until then, hiding is reversible and the content and authorship are preserved. Restoring content ends its hidden state; a later hide starts a new hidden period.

### Your choices and requests

You choose what you post in the Community and which saved material you submit for editorial input. You can use the Studio’s available export tools to take copies of your work. Contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or to ask about restrictions or other rights available under the law that applies to you. We may need to verify your identity and explain any information that must be retained. Account closure, export and a privacy request are different actions; asking a question does not itself close your account.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **hello@tidesofknowing.com**. **[OWNER REVIEW: confirm the final contact, response process and any applicable complaint/regulator information.]**
