# Explore and paid entitlement reconciliation — review only

Status: **not implemented, not migration-ready, not authorised for hosted execution**. This specification preserves the prior Explore migration, source, programme records and historical retention evidence. It does not authorise deletion, reminder sending, Stripe changes or any grant mutation.

## Verified contract and gap

- `studio_access` has one row per user, with `intro` or `paid` phase and one start/end interval. `studio_private.has_access()` reads that projection. The billing code `projectPaidAccess` can derive a current projection from multiple verified windows and an original manual baseline, but a single row does not independently prove the source, freshness or completeness of every overlapping entitlement. Do not reinterpret old rows or overwrite their baselines.
- The installed `studio_private.owner_download_grace_v1()` currently checks an active profile without an expiry boundary. `effectiveStudioReadAccess` similarly allows an active owner without a 28-day cutoff. Existing RLS consumers include project, card, history, editorial and private journal Storage policies. A helper-only change is insufficient: project selection, exports and several application routes have additional independent gates.
- The local Explore migration treats any active profile as eligible for creator writes. Its capacity trigger deliberately allows ordinary edits to an already active project/card after paid expiry, including over-capacity work. Project-level classification is not recorded, so this must not be deployed as the final expiry boundary.
- `studioPaidAccessState` implements and tests the 28-day state of the existing **single projected grant**. It is used by the existing paid-access check for active status; it does not currently enforce project retrieval, modify RLS, establish a free-project binding, or prove all grants were projected correctly.

## Required decisions and authoritative data

1. Define how a member selects or establishes their one Explore project when paid work already exists. In particular, do not automatically convert an over-ten-card project, delete cards, archive work or move content. The owner must decide the permitted transition for that project. The permanent free Explore entitlement must remain usable independently of paid expiry.
2. Identify an authoritative, versioned set of independently valid Create-or-higher grants, including existing manual/Founding Creator grants and verified programme or subscription periods. Preserve all original promises, revocations, administrator decisions and billing baselines. Existing commercial records must be mapped and reconciled; no guessed backfill or newly activated entitlement is authorised. The current `studio_access` projection may be retained for compatibility only if its derivation, freshness and atomic updates are proven for all sources, gaps, overlaps, cancellations and renewal events.
3. Define a trusted, owner-scoped binding distinguishing the explicitly eligible Explore project from retained paid projects. It must be database-controlled, transactionally consistent with capacity checks and impossible for a browser to forge through editable project settings. Historical paid content and original ownership must remain unchanged. Do not classify unknown historical projects by count alone.
4. Resolve the precise handling of revoked, refunded and disputed grants using existing contractual decisions before treating them as ordinary expiry. Unknown source, incomplete provenance, stale projection or ambiguous project classification must fail closed for paid-only writes without deleting work or cancelling registered Explore eligibility.

## Minimum implementation contract for review

- Separate **account eligibility**, **current paid authority**, **project-specific Explore editing**, and **paid-project read/download**. An active registered profile can retain Explore eligibility even when no paid grant is active. An active, verified Create-or-higher grant takes precedence regardless of another programme's expiry.
- For expired paid work, permit owner-bound retrieval from the verified applicable expiry until, but excluding, expiry plus 28 days. During that window, the expired grant provides no editing or programme-service rights. Active independently valid rights take precedence; future-only grants do not bridge a gap. Explicitly classified Explore work remains accessible under the approved free scope independently of paid expiry.
- For unknown or over-capacity historical projects, preserve all data and ownership; do not treat the project as an active free workspace without the approved transition and capacity check. Refused writes and allocations must be transactional and preserve drafts, versions, Guidebook, Workbook, stored media, service records and other existing work.
- Apply the same project-aware decision to application pages, mutation and export endpoints, snapshot/export RPCs, all relevant RLS policies and private Storage objects. Revalidate existing helper consumers and exact installed policy definitions before changing them. Avoid broad account-level SELECT or write grants, editable JSON-based privilege flags, client-side-only checks and public/service-role credentials in browser code.
- Any schema migration must be a separate, guarded review candidate with explicit rollback that never destroys classification or creative data. Existing grants and commercial records remain untouched unless a separately approved, evidence-backed reconciliation specifies otherwise. No retention schedule or destructive operation is included.

In particular, the current projected row can be stale or omit a valid overlapping entitlement; the candidate does not prove complete Create/Collaborate/Develop/Founding Creator precedence.

When paid access restores or edits a previously bound Explore project, the paid-write marker prevents its automatic conversion after expiry; another unchanged Explore binding can remain eligible. If reconciliation nevertheless finds multiple active, unmarked Explore bindings, the candidate fails closed and preserves them rather than selecting or deleting a project. A non-destructive owner-approved transition is still required. The precise treatment of former paid projects at or below ten cards remains a future approval; over-capacity projects are not silently converted.

The synthetic fixture verifies that its owner can create a separate free project while the expanded project remains intact and read-only during paid grace. This conservative marker does not prove complete historical paid provenance: pre-migration projects remain unbound, and an independently verified entitlement/source reconciliation is still required.
