# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

## Who is responsible for your information

Tides of Knowing, operated by Leilia Spencer, is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **hello@tidesofknowing.com**.

This policy covers the Tides of Knowing website, Ask Leilia, the COMPASS programme, Deck Creator Studio (DCS), the Studio Community and related support services.

## Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you register, contact us, apply for a programme, request a reading, purchase a service or use the Studio. Depending on the service, this may include:

- Your name, email address, Community username and correspondence.
- Account, authentication, membership and access records.
- Applications, reading questions, consultation requests and scheduling information.
- Project material you choose to save, including ideas, card text, artwork, reference images, guidebook material, workbook responses, plans and private journal entries.
- Community contributions, replies, Scribes, reports and moderation records.
- Material you deliberately submit for confidential editorial feedback.
- Transaction references and records of agreed programme benefits and services.

Please share only the personal information needed for your question or project. Creative work and reading questions can reveal sensitive information about you or other people. Avoid including unnecessary health details, identification documents or other sensitive material.

Our hosting and authentication providers also process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as network addresses, browser information, requests, errors and sign-in activity.

## How we use your information

We use information to:

- Operate the website and Studio, authenticate accounts and protect access.
- Save your work and provide the available editing, preview and export tools.
- Deliver readings, programmes and services you request.
- Respond to enquiries and arrange agreed sessions.
- Administer memberships, complimentary access and programme allowances.
- Operate and moderate the Community.
- Investigate reports, prevent abuse and resolve technical problems.
- Maintain necessary business, security and recovery records.

Service messages include information needed to manage your account or deliver something you requested. We will seek any required permission before using your information for a separate marketing purpose, and you can ask to stop receiving marketing messages.

We do not sell or rent your personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## Your project and ownership

Your private Studio project is separate from anything you choose to share in the Community.

Other creators do not receive access to your project merely because you use the Studio or participate in the Community. Authorised staff and service providers may need limited access to operate the service, investigate a problem, provide support you request or meet a legal obligation.

Your ideas, writing, guidebook and artwork remain yours. Using the Studio, its tools or its guidance does not transfer ownership of your work to Tides of Knowing or DCS.

We do not treat your use of the Studio as permission to use your private project material to train AI models. Before introducing an optional feature that sends your material to an AI provider, we will explain what is sent, why, and what choices you have.

## The Studio Community

All pathways share the same Studio Community.

When you contribute, your Community username, contribution and any applicable pathway or Founder badge can be visible to eligible Community members. Your account email address is not displayed as your Community identity.

Reports are directed to the authorised moderator rather than published as Community contributions. Moderation records may include the reported item, the reason, the decision, the person who acted and the relevant dates.

We also maintain administrative task records, including read state and completion history. Marking an administrative task complete does not itself hide or delete the contribution.

Community members may retain information they have already seen. Please consider what you share and avoid posting confidential project material or another person's private information without permission.

## Confidential editorial support

Confidential editorial requests are separate from the Community.

We receive the saved version, selected passage or reference preview, question and context you explicitly submit. Later project edits do not automatically replace that submitted snapshot.

Access is restricted to you and the authorised editorial recipient, subject to necessary technical support and legal requirements. Submitting material does not grant artists or other creators access to your project.

An assistant or collaborator does not automatically receive access to your private material. Where an agreed service involves an assistant, we will explain the necessary access beforehand and require appropriate confidentiality arrangements.

## Artists and other collaborators

You decide what to share with artists, designers, illustrators, co-authors and other collaborators.

Information you send them directly is also subject to their own privacy practices. Your agreements, permissions and payments with them are separate from your use of the Studio.

We will not give a collaborator access to your private project simply because they appear in an artist directory or are mentioned in your work.

## Service providers and international processing

We use service providers to deliver the website and Studio, including:

- **Cloudflare** for website delivery and application hosting.
- **Supabase** for database services, authentication and private file storage.
- **Stripe**, where payment collection is available, for payment processing.

Our current DCS database region is Singapore. Hosting, delivery and provider support may involve processing in other countries.

We limit provider access to what is needed for the service and require appropriate safeguards. Where additional consent or another legal condition is required for a transfer, we will address that before proceeding.

We may disclose information where necessary to meet a legal obligation, respond to a valid legal process or protect people and the service.

## Payments

Payment providers process payment-card information. We do not store full payment-card details in the Studio.

We may retain transaction references, invoices and related access records to administer purchases, resolve payment issues and meet accounting obligations.

This policy does not activate DCS payment collection or change any agreed commercial terms.

## Cookies and browser storage

The website and Studio use cookies or similar browser storage where needed for authentication, security and service operation. Some tools may retain a working draft in your browser to help you recover unsent writing.

You can manage browser storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in or draft recovery.

Any optional analytics or advertising technologies must be identified separately, with the choices or consent required for their use.

## How long we keep information

We keep information for the purposes explained in this policy, including delivering agreed services, maintaining access, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations.

Ending a membership does not automatically delete an account or project. You can contact us to request account closure or deletion and to discuss exporting your work first.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymise it, except where a justified legal, security or record-keeping requirement applies. Information retained for those reasons will have restricted access.

### Hidden or withdrawn Community content

Hidden or withdrawn Community content will normally be retained for **12 months from the date it was hidden or withdrawn**, then permanently deleted through a controlled retention process, unless a report, dispute or legal obligation requires longer retention.

Hiding is reversible during that period. Restoring an item ends its hidden state; hiding it again starts a new hidden period.

### Reported content

We retain reported content while its report is open and for **24 months after the report's final resolution**. If a report is reopened, the period runs from its most recent resolution.

If a report is dismissed and the contribution remains published, the 24-month period applies to the report details. The published contribution is not deleted under this rule.

Reported content will not be deleted before any applicable 12-month hidden-content retention period has also ended.

Relevant records may be kept longer where necessary for an active dispute, investigation or legal obligation. We review that additional retention when the matter ends and remove information that is no longer needed.

After the applicable retention period, reported content and associated report details are permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymised. We may retain a limited moderation record showing the decision, date, reason and responsible administrator, without retaining the deleted contribution's full text.

### Moderation and task history

We may retain a limited record of an action, its reason, date and responsible administrator after the underlying content is removed. Such records should contain only the information needed for accountability and should avoid unnecessarily reproducing deleted content.

### Backups

Backups have their own restricted access and retention arrangements. Removing information from the live service does not immediately remove every backup copy. If a backup is restored, relevant deletion decisions must be reapplied.

Automatic cleanup follows these retention periods. Open reports and discussions marked to be kept for a dispute, legal hold or other preservation need are excluded. Minimal structural records remain so moderation and task history can still be understood.

## Security and confidentiality

We use access controls and other administrative and technical measures to protect information. Access should be limited to authorised people who need it for their work.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. If a security incident affects your information, we will investigate, take appropriate action and provide any notices required by law.

## Your privacy rights and requests

You may request access to, correction of, cancellation of or opposition to the processing of your personal information. These are commonly called **ARCO rights** in Mexico.

Email **hello@tidesofknowing.com** with your name, the request and enough information to locate the relevant records. We will explain how to verify your identity securely; do not send passwords or account keys.

We will respond within applicable legal timeframes, explain any permitted extension or retention requirement, and tell you the outcome. You may also request withdrawal of consent where applicable. These rights are addressed in Mexico's [Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf).

A privacy enquiry does not itself close your account. If a request affects our ability to provide a service, we will explain that before taking action.

## Changes to this policy

We will publish updates on the website and change the effective date. Where a material change requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it before applying that change.
