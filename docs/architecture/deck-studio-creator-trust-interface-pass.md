# Deck Creator Studio: creator trust and interface pass

Status: product and implementation requirements for the isolated Studio feature branch. No public ownership, retention, deletion or refund promise should be expanded until its corresponding behaviour and terms have been approved and verified.

## Infrastructure and creator control

1. **Ownership boundary.** State plainly in Studio-specific terms that creators retain rights in their original manuscripts, card text, artwork, photographs, research and other material they contribute. Define the limited permission Tides needs to store, process, back up and display that material to provide the service. Distinguish creator material from Tides' programme materials and from work licensed by an artist or other collaborator. Public sharing and human review require their own explicit permissions. The existing COMPASS intellectual-property clause in `/terms/` concerns programme materials and needs a clear Studio carve-out before launch.
2. **Dated work record.** Preserve server-side creation and modification times and version history for manuscripts and records, including authorship and export metadata. Make the history understandable to the creator. A timestamp is evidence of a particular saved state; it must never be presented as a registration, a worldwide copyright grant or conclusive proof of authorship.
3. **One project-folder download from the account page.** Give the creator a single, visible export action for each project, available while editing or during the published read-only access period. Package the project description, card families and ordered cards, all current guidebook sections, available version histories, journal and excerpt content, production plan, source/credit metadata and associated original media where authorised. Use readable Markdown plus structured JSON and a manifest recording export time, file inventory and omissions. Avoid platform-only IDs as the sole way to understand relationships. Include a successful export test on an imported full deck and a creator-only access test. Existing per-project Markdown/JSON exports are a useful base, but they do not constitute the complete project folder.
4. **Exit and deletion.** Decide and publish when editing stops, how long the creator can sign in and export, the deadline for requesting an extension or rejoining, and when each class of live data and backup becomes unavailable or is deleted. Current code defaults to 30 days of read-only access for some subscription endings, permits an unset read-only end in other states, and contains no scheduled deletion or warning-email workflow. Do not promise automatic deletion or a three-month reminder until these processes exist. A rejoin invitation must be separate from essential deletion notices and subject to the person's communication preferences.
5. **Refund decision.** No Studio-specific 30-day money back guarantee is defined in the current offer, billing code or terms. The `/terms/` page has a COMPASS rule allowing refunds until 48 hours before the programme and none after it starts. Decide whether Studio, Guided Deck Circle and Private Deck Partnership each have a distinct first-payment guarantee, what use or service conditions apply, and how cancellation, access and the export window work after a refund. Publish one consistent policy at the offer, before Stripe checkout, in account settings and in Studio terms; test the refund and entitlement path in Stripe test mode. Do not imply that cancellation automatically issues a refund.
6. **Billing reliability.** Process signed Stripe events with checked database writes and safe retries. A ledger row marked completed must mean every required entitlement and reservation effect succeeded. A failed or interrupted delivery must remain recoverable without double-counting invoices or incorrectly revoking access when events arrive out of order. Validate the six subscribed event types, repeated deliveries and missing metadata in isolated staging before production.

## Copy and layout

After the infrastructure contracts are settled, review the Studio offer, enrolment, onboarding, project dashboard, account and exit states as a continuous creator journey. Keep the approved warm-paper, ocean-ink and restrained-gold editorial direction within Tides of Knowing. Explain ownership, privacy, access after cancellation, export and refund terms in ordinary language with direct links to the corresponding actions. Check text contrast, field labels, empty states, mobile layout and confirmation/error copy against real creator accounts.

## Stage 9 boundary

Continue the staging creator journey and billing lifecycle now. Track this pass alongside the release gate, and complete the ownership, exit, export and payment disclosures before paid public launch. Production `main`, live Stripe and production Supabase are outside this pass until the release gate is approved.
